A Little About Me

Hi, I'm Alan!

I'm an author and screenwriter, and lifelong lover of stories. A love for exploring the extraordinary within the ordinary. I'm drawn to stories that explore life's hardships, its light-hearted and humorous moments, the strength of the human heart, and the importance of hope.

So, why Stories by Alan?

As someone who grew up watching soap-operas, which eventually became an obsession of all forms of serialized content, I believe there's something magical about experiencing a story as it unfolds. The anticipation between chapters, the lingering questions, the characters that occupy your thoughts long after you've stopped reading.

I wanted to create a little corner of the internet dedicated to that experience. A home for my stories, where I can share my fiction with readers one chapter at a time.

Here, you'll find stories across different genres, worlds both familiar and strange, and characters navigating all the wonderful, complicated things that make us human.

So, find a comfortable spot, grab your favorite drink, and make yourself at home.

There's always another story waiting to be told.

Happy reading,

Alan
