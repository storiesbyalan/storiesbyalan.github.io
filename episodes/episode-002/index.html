JAD: … there needs to be a balance. We can’t alienate our audience.

JJ: But dad-

Jad raises his palm cutting JJ off.

HADDAD MEDIA — CONFERENCE ROOM

YOUSSEF: … You know as well as anyone that there’s never a guarantee in this business, and -

Marwan cuts Youssef off, his voice stern.

MARWAN: With all due respect, you haven’t seen what I’ve had the privilege to see. The film is unlike anything this country has seen before.

DIVIDER

J&R STUDIOS — JAD'S OFFICE

JJ: Well, let’s just hope we can trust whoever they are enough to do that.

JJ glares his father. His face red boils with rage.

He turns to meet the eyes of the person standing in front of him:

Amir.

BEIRUT — J&R STUDIOS — JAD’S OFFICE

JJ glares at Amir, anger radiating from him. Amir looking from JJ to Jad, picks up that something feels off, almost as if he wasn’t expected, although he knew he was.

AMIR: I’m sorry, I didn’t mean to interrupt.

JAD: Not at all.

Amir smiles and nods, then looks towards JJ.

AMIR: Hi…

JJ does not respond, only continues to glare.

JAD: I was just mentioning you to JJ.

JJ: Except you didn’t. You only said that you were bringing someone in who wasn’t a stranger.

JAD: Exactly.

Jad narrows his eyes at JJ, drawing a line.

JAD: Please, come in.

Amir and JJ’s eyes meet, tension cuttable with a knife.

AMIR: Um, do you mind…

JJ steps to the side as Amir uncomfortably walks past him towards one of the chairs facing Jad’s desk.

AMIR: Again, I’m sorry. I didn’t mean to barge in. Your assistant called and she said you wanted to see me. Is this regarding the offer?

JAD: Yes, I wanted to know whether or not you’d given it some thought.

AMIR: I have, sir.

JAD: And?

AMIR: I’d like to accept your offer. I’d be honored to work for J&R Studios.

JAD: Glad to hear it! You’ll be working alongside JJ, helping him fine-tune and reassess the department I set up for him.

JJ: Like hell he will.

Amir turns towards JJ, brows furrowed in confusion. JJ stares down his father with no sign of backing down.

DIVIDER

J&R STUDIOS — JJ’S OFFICE

JJ sits at his desk, his knee aggressively bobs up and down. He stars at the box office numbers on his computer screen. Numbers are still in the red.

JJ: Damn it.

A knock sounds on the glass door. JJ flits his eyes towards it. Amir stands on the other side.

JJ exhales and shakes his head.

The hell does he want?

Amir opens the door and walks in.

JJ: I didn’t say you could come in.

AMIR: Cut the crap, JJ.

JJ: Excuse me?

JJ stands up and walks towards Amir.

AMIR: There’s no way we’re going to be able to work together if you’re going to be like this.

JJ: You’re delusional.

AMIR: Wait… Is this because of -

JJ’s arm shoots up, palm facing Amir.

JJ: Don’t! Just don’t.

JJ turns away from Amir.

AMIR: It was years ago. I didn’t realize you -

JJ: I said don’t!

AMIR: Fine, but I’m not giving up this job, JJ. I really think we can move past this and do great things together.

JJ: Can you leave, please?

Amir nods, closes his eyes, and heads out the door.

JJ lowers his head, takes a deep breath trying to stop himself from crying.
