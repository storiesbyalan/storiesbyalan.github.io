EPISODE 002

PREVIOUSLY ON

BEIRUT
J&R STUDIOS, JAD’S OFFICE

JJ tilts his head. Brow furrows.

JAD
This is a big undertaking, especially given your responsibilities as the vice president of the company. I still need you by my side.

JJ
Of course, dad. I can handle it.

DOWNTOWN BEIRUT
AMIR’S APARTMENT 

SAMIRA
Are you going to see them?

Samira picks up a small brass HM monogram mounted on an obsidian stone. The monogram sits above the full HADDAD MEDIA name

AMIR
No. The last thing I want to do right now is see my dad, or any of them for that matter.

J&R STUDIOS
JESS'S OFFICE

JJ throws his hands in the air.

He's spiraling. Denying it no longer mattered.

JJ
He kicked me out of his office.

JESS
JJ, breathe. Whatever dad is or isn't doing, I'm sure he has his reasons.

J&R STUDIOS
JAD’S OFFICE

Jad smiles. 

JAD
I want to offer you a job. Here. At J&R Studios.

— the office door swings open and JJ walks in.

Amir turns around. JJ sees him. Freezes in the doorway.

AMIR
Hi...

BEIRUT
HADDAD MEDIA

Marwan Haddad strides down the hall of Haddad Media's Executive Floor in a sharply tailored suit. 34. Effortlessly oozes confidence and polished charm. Brown hair. Neatly trimmed beard. Handsome in a clean, classical way.

He makes a pit stop at the receptionist’s desk.

A young woman, couldn’t be older than 22, returns the phone handset to its cradle. Despite her skin being slightly tan, pink still blooms on her cheeks when she looks up and sees Marwan standing before her.

BLUSHING RECEPTIONIST
Mr. Haddad…

MARWAN
Hey, Romi, any news about his arrival?

ROMI
Nothing yet, sir.

MARWAN
Alright, please keep me updated. I want to know the moment you hear anything.

ROMI
Of course.

MARWAN
Thank you.

Marwan turns and heads towards…

EVP, STRATEGY & ACQUISITION OFFICE

He gives the door a rhythmic knock. A “Come in” from within, and he opens it.

MARWAN
Hey.

A woman clearly much younger than Marwan sits behind the desk. Her brown hair shoulder-length falls in sleek lines around her face. She has refined features. Defined cheekbones. Sharp, attentive eyes.

She is Sasha Haddad. Marwan's sister and Marwan’s sister and Haddad Media’s Executive Vice President of Strategy and Acquisitions.

Sasha wears a dark tailored blazer, with an understated top underneath. Polished rather than fancy. Minimal jewelry. Elegant enough for the executive floor, with the quiet certainty of someone who earned her place there

She doesn't take her eyes away from the stack of contracts she studies.

SASHA
Hey.

MARWAN
Busy?

SASHA
What does it look like?

MARWAN
Well, I would come back later but every second counts here. 

Sasha sighs. She places her pen on the exact line she was reading. Clasps her hands and fixes her eyes on Marwan. 

SASHA
I assume this is about dad?

MARWAN
You know he's coming back to work today?

SASHA
Yes.

MARWAN
Do you really think it's a good idea?

SASHA
Regardless of what we think, have you ever known dad to listen to anyone but himself?

MARWAN
I guess not. I just want to make sure he's going to be able to handle things.

Sasha arches her brow. Leans forward on her desk.

SASHA
Look he got out almost three weeks ago.

MARWAN
Sasha, he almost died.

SASHA
But he didn't. I'm sure he wouldn't be coming back if his doctors didn't approve it.

Marwan shakes his head in disbelief.

MARWAN
Are we talking about the same man here?

SASHA
What do you expect us to do about it then? Lock him out of the building?

MARWAN
No, but maybe we should call mom and have her try to convince him to stay home. 

SASHA
You know as well as I do that she's tried and failed. There's nothing we can do at this point except be ready when he arrives.

MARWAN
So, that's it then?

Sasha shrugs.

SASHA
Listen, I'm sorry but I really don't have time for this right now. I'm trying to reach Levantel and I have a call with Montclare Pictures and then Netflix.

MARWAN
And the board meeting later.

SASHA
Exactly.

MARWAN
Alright. I'll leave you to it then.

SASHA
Thanks. Please, shut the door—

Marwan throws his thumb up in the air as he leaves the office closing the door behind him. 

J&R STUDIOS
EXECUTIVE FLOOR, JAD'S OFFICE

AMIR
Hi...

Amir stands. JJ's eyes follow Amir. No other part of his body moves.

JAD
JJ, I'm glad you're here. You should probably be here for this anyway.

Still no movement.

JAD
Please come in, son.

Amir's eyes meet JJ's.

He recognizes that look. The little tightening of the jaw.

Damn. This won't be good.

JAD
JJ?

Finally, the ice around JJ cracks.

He looks at his father. Nods. Cautiously walks over to the other chair near Amir and sits.

Both Amir and Jad follow.

Silence drowns the office.

JJ's eyes slip from his father to the person beside him. His gaze lingers a fraction longer than expected.

JJ
What is he doing here?

JAD
Well, I was offering Amir a position here at J&R Studios.

JJ shoots his eyes towards Jad. 

JJ
You what?

JAD
I was about to tell Amir about the position I want to hire him for.

JJ
You're giving him a job at J&R Studios? Him?

Jad leans back in his chair.

JAD
Why wouldn't I?

JJ
You're serious?

AMIR
Look if this is about my family —

JJ's eyes don't break away from his father.

JJ
Of course it's about your family! Dad, what are you thinking?

JAD
I'm thinking that we need someone new. Fresh blood. Someone who can help take us in a brand new direction.

JJ
What about who he is?

AMIR
Can we please not talk about me while I'm sitting right here?

JJ looks down for a beat. Then away. Toward the window.

AMIR
Look I understand JJ's concerns and I'd be surprised if he didn't have any. But I want nothing to do with my family. Nor my dad's business. Trust me.

JJ laughs under his breath. Almost impressed by the absurdity of what Amir just said.

JAD
I appreciate your candor. But your background is exactly why I'm hiring you in the first place. 

JJ eyes shoot towards Jad. His father's plans becoming clearer to him by the second.

JAD
You being here, at J&R Studios, will give us an advantage. You know how your family works. You know the ins and outs of Haddad Media. You can help us strategize. Be one step ahead.

Amir tilts his head. A half-smile threatening. Interested. Wary.

JAD
I want you here because not only are you worthy of being a part of J&R, but also because you're a chess piece that no one will see coming...

Jad leans in. Rests his arms on his desk. 

JAD
Not to mention, the look on your father's face.

Amir’s half-smile breaks into something fuller.

He looks to JJ. Concern stares back at him. He then turns to Jad, who’s already smiling.

JAD
So, shall I draw up the contract?

HADDAD MEDIA
GEORGE HADDAD'S OFFICE

George Haddad looks good for being 60, and someone who recently was discharged from the hospital. Silver hair. White beard. Dark eyes deep-set beneath heavy brows.

There's a reserved yet commanding quality to him. As there should be given that he's the founder and CEO of Haddad Media.

He plants a hand on his desk. Sets his brief case down. Slowly, lifts back up.

A woman's voice, warm and raspy giving away her age, sneaks into the office.

OLDER WOMAN'S VOICE
Two weeks out of the hospital and you're already determined to pretend otherwise.

George turns to see his assistant, Hanadi carrying a stack of folders.

He chuckles.

GEORGE
Hello, to you too. Hanadi. It sure is nice to see you.

Hanadi smiles.

Mid-50s. Silver threaded through dark hair. Observant eyes. Round face. Full-figured and impeccably put together. The kind of woman who notices everything and reacts to very little.

She's worked for George long enough to read his moods before most people can. No wonder she wasn't surprised to see him at work.

HANADI
How are you?

GEORGE
Better than expected. Doctors gave me a clean bill of health.

George walks to his chair and 

HANADI
There's no surprise there. They did give you a pacemaker. You're practically brand new.

GEORGE
Practically. Dina doesn't seem to think so. She's still huffing and —

HANADI
I'll not have you criticizing your wonderful wife. She's doing the Lord's work with you.

GEORGE
Yeah, well. I couldn't stay another day at home. I needed to come back.

George circles behind the desk. One hand briefly settles against its edge before he picks up a stack of papers.

He skims the first page. Attention seemingly divided.

GEORGE
Have we heard anything from Ayman over at Levantel?

George sits.

HANADI
I was actually wanting to mention this to you. He called this morning. Said he wants to revisit the premiere agreement.

George's eyes break from the paper and shoots his head up to Hanadi.

GEORGE
Revisit what?

HANADI
Levantel's commitment to the premiere. I think he wants to —

George slams his palm onto his desk. Sharp. Controlled. Yet, loud enough to make Hanadi jump.

GEORGE
That boy has no business running Levantel. I warned Rafik about his decision in putting Ayman in charge.

George leans back slightly, jaw tightening.

GEORGE
Is Sasha in her office?

HANADI
I'm pretty sure she is. 

GEORGE
Give her a ring and tell her I'll be heading down to see her.

HANADI
Sure. 

George rises from his chair. The room tilts hard. Vision swims. Knees buckle. He stumbles. His hand misses the desk.

HANADI
George!

ACT BREAK

HADDAD MEDIA
GEORGE HADDAD'S OFFICE

Hanadi stands at George's side. Helps him up. Slowly. Cautiously.

HANADI
Are you alright?

GEORGE
Yes. Yes. Fine.

HANADI
George...

George balances himself. Takes a deep breath in. Looks around and nods. 

GEORGE
I'm fine, Hanadi.

HANADI
Look, maybe you should—

GEORGE
I said I'm fine!

Hanadi takes a step back. Unfazed. She knows when George can be reasoned with.

This isn’t it.

HANADI
I'll go let Sasha know you're on your way. 

GEORGE
I'll see her at the board meeting.

George makes his way back to the chair. Slowly, lowers himself and sits. 

GEORGE
What time is the meeting?

HANADI
In about an hour. 

GEORGE
Thank you. 

Hanadi nods and turns to head back to her desk. 

GEORGE
Hanadi...

She stops at the door. 

GEORGE
Not a word of this...

Hanadi closes her eyes. Stubborn man. 

GEORGE
...to anyone.

She walks out of the office and closes the door behind her.

J&R STUDIOS
EXECUTIVE FLOOR, JAD'S OFFICE

JAD
I can call HR right now and draw up the contract. It could be here in minutes just say the word.

JJ
Dad, are you really going to do this?

Jad raises his hand. Palm facing JJ. 

AMIR
