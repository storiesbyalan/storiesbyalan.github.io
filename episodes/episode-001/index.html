EPISODE 001

AIRPLANE
OVER THE MEDITERRANEAN

The inside of the plane is dark, not entirely quite. A young child laughs somewhere in the back rows. A woman coughs.

DING.

An older passenger requests one of the flight attendants. Beside him sits a handsome young man with a military buzz cut and a short beard lays his head against the window.

The flight attendant walks up to the older man.

FLIGHT ATTENDANT
Anything you need, sir?

Her voice warm and soft.

OLDER PASSENGER
Just a cup of water please.

FLIGHT ATTENDANT
Of course.

The flight attendant looks over towards the young man.

FLIGHT ATTENDANT
Anything for you, sir?

The young man lost in a daze doesn’t realize she’s speaking to him. The older man nudges his neighbor with his elbow causing the young man to snap out of his daydream.

He looks towards the flight attendant, who gives him a smile.

FLIGHT ATTENDANT
Did you need anything as well sir?

YOUNG MAN
Oh, no thank you.

The flight attendant nods and makes her way to her station to fulfill the older man’s request.

PILOT’S VOICE
Ladies and gentlemen, we’ve just begun our descent into Beirut. We should be landing in about twenty minutes. The weather on the ground is warm and clear. On behalf of the entire crew, thank you for flying with us, and welcome home to those of you returning.

The older man looks over to the younger man, who looks out the window.

OLDER MAN
How long has it been?

The young man turns towards the man sitting next to him, a quizzical look on his face.

OLDER MAN
How long has it been since you’ve been back?

YOUNG MAN
Oh, um, three years. I actually never thought I’d be back.

The young man turns towards the window again, takes a deep breath, then exhales.

Worry swimming from his mind to his eyes, as he watches Beirut inch closer and closer as the plane makes its way to the runway.

LEBANON
RAFIK AL HARIRI AIRPORT

It’s midday.

The harsh summer sun blazes down calling forth heat shimmers.

Traffic and the sound of horns pollute the entrance to the airport. Taxi drivers stand with their backs laid on their cars waving down arrivals asking whether or not those walking out of the airport need a ride. Some take their offer. Others shake their heads or raise their palms. A clear rejection.

The last time Amir was at the airport he was leaving. Now, three years later, he walks out of the airport. He was 28 when he left Lebanon. Three years later, he returns with a backpack and a big duffle bag. 

Amir stops, sets the bag between his feet, and pulls out his phone, as other arrivals walk past him.

He dials a number then brings the mobile to his ear.

AMIR
Hey, it’s me… Yeah, I just landed… Flight wasn't bad… Would you please tell him that I arrived? Thanks… Bye.

A nervous smile appears on his face.

He puts his mobile back in his pocket, picks up the duffle bag.

AMIR
Here goes nothing.

Amir shakes his head as he heads towards a taxi driver.

BEIRUT
J&R STUDIOS, JAD’S OFFICE

Jad Njeim sits behind his desk, looking through proposals. His piercing blue eyes scan the pages before him. He’s an unconventionally handsome 60-year-old man with curly graying hair and a matching trimmed beard.

Despite being the CEO and co-founder of J&R Studios, he wears a sky blue button-down shirt with rolled up sleeves, and fitted black jeans.

Jad’s office, located on the Executive Floor, is minimalistic, organized: only a few posters hang on the walls near a small shelf with a couple of books. A laptop sits on the desk, next to it is a stack of scripts, Jad’s mobile phone, and a family portrait in a simple black frame.

Three quick knocks on the door.

JAD
Come in.

A young man swings the door open and leans into the office.

YOUNG MAN
Hey dad. Carla told me you wanted to see me.

Jad looks up and sets the stapled papers he was looking through aside.

JAD
JJ! Yes, come in.

JJ walks into the office and closes the door behind him. He makes his way to one of the two chairs that face Jad's desk. Sits.

He looks young for his age, newly 30. A mix of Jad and his mother. Structurally, he has his dad’s curly hair and sharp features, though JJ’s are a touch softer. His hazel eyes, however, are a carbon copy of his mother’s. He dons an auburn mullet, and an earring in each ear.

JJ
So, what's up?

JAD
I wanted to discuss the launch of the new program. I know how hard you and your sister have been working on it.

JJ
Honestly, when Jess and I pitched this project to you a month ago, I didn't realize we'd move so fast.

JAD
It was the perfect time, especially since productions have stalled. I know the Haddad's are about to release their latest film, so I wanted to make sure that J&R continues to be part of the discourse and online chatter.

JJ
From what I hear, it's their biggest budget yet.

JAD
Well, that's exactly why I decided to move as fast as we did to establish J&R's New Voices Program. Are we finalizing everything we need before we launch?

JJ
Yes, I believe so. Just some final touches. I know Jess has already reached out to the press. Mom's prepping the space, and I'm going through the application process to make sure it's ready for applicants.

JAD
Good, good.

JJ smiles and nods. He straightens his back. For the first time in a long time, a sense of pride blooms within him.

JAD
I am worried about one thing, though. 

JJ
Oh?

JJ tilts his head. Brow furrows. The pride he felt mere seconds ago dissipating.

JAD
This is a big undertaking, especially given your responsibilities as the vice president of the company. I still need you by my side.

JJ
Of course, dad. I can handle it.

JAD
Well, I'm simply reminding you that, despite how hard you worked on this project, your priority should remain with your responsibilities as VP.

JJ
Yeah, but dad, this is something Jess and I built from the ground up. I can't not be involved when it launches.

JAD
We can discuss this further later. I have a meeting coming up and I have a few things I need to finish.

JJ
Ok, but I'm not going to back down, dad.

JAD
I said we can discuss this later.

Jad almost glares at JJ.

JJ
Fine.

He stands and leaves the office, closing the door behind him.

JJ tilts his head down. This was not how he expected this conversation to go.

JJ
Damn it...

He turns and heads down the hall.

DOWNTOWN BEIRUT
AMIR’S APARTMENT 

Amir opens the door to his apartment.

Sighs, exhaustion washing over him.

Traffic was a killer.

He flicks the light switch on. The lights flicker for a moment before stabilizing.

The apartment is just as he left it three years ago: a living, breathing collage of bohemian and antique Lebanese treasures. Each piece embracing its own history, like they’d traveled a long way to end up right here in this space.

The only difference is how dusty it’s gotten. The wooden coffee table, worn and nicked from years of being handed down and sold off, sits near an old armchair with faded embroidery.

AMIR
Home sweet home.

Amir’s smile fights back against his exhausted state.

AMIR
Good to be back.

Amir closes the door and walks to his bedroom, his bags forcing him to squeeze through the threshold. He drops them near the bed, grabs his mobile and starts playing the latest Stray Kids album. Nothing like feel good K-Pop music.

Amir unzips the duffle bag. Lays his clothes on the bed in small vertical piles, singing along and dancing to the music.

A knock on the door interrupts him.

He pauses the music. Furrows his brow.

Strange... nobody was supposed to know he was back.

Amir reaches the door.

AMIR
Who is it?

WOMAN’S VOICE
Bitch, open the door.

Amir immediately rolls his eyes. Swings the door open revealing a young woman.

If her demand wasn’t recognizable enough, her appearance definitely was. Bushy curly hair, three earrings on one ear and one hanging on the other.

Eclectic clothing attires that were taken straight of the 1960’s. A whiskey bottle in her right hand.

AMIR
How the hell did you know I’m back, Samira?

Samira glares at Amir in utter disbelief. She pushes past him walking into the apartment.

SAMIRA
Usually if your best friend decides to come back home, they’d let you know beforehand. I guess that doesn’t apply to us?

AMIR
You didn’t answer my question…

Amir closes the door.

SAMIRA
You didn’t answer mine.

Samira sets the whiskey bottle on the table in the living room.

AMIR
Samira...

SAMIRA
Amir…

Amir narrows his eyes. He knows Samira’s mocking tone all too well.

AMIR
What are you doing here?

SAMIRA
Why didn’t you tell me you were coming back?

AMIR
I didn’t tell anyone I was coming back.

Samira crosses her arms.

SAMIRA
Bitch, I’m not anyone.

Amir sighs. A soft smile sprinkled with guilt forms on his face.

AMIR
Ok, you’re right. I’m sorry. I should’ve told you, but honestly it happened really fast. I wasn’t expecting to come back, hell I wasn’t even thinking about it after everything that happened with my family.

SAMIRA
Are you going to see them?

Samira picks up a small brass HM monogram mounted on an obsidian stone. The monogram sits above the full HADDAD MEDIA name

AMIR
No. The last thing I want to do right now is see my dad, or any of them for that matter.

SAMIRA
Even your mom?

Amir pauses. Looks away from Samira.

His mom was sore spot. Any mention of her always stung.

AMIR
I didn't really leave on the best of terms. We've barely spoken since.

SAMIRA
She was devastated when you left, you know that right?

AMIR
She sure had a way of showing it. Mom was the only one that checked in with me but even then, it was like once a month. Anyway, can we change the subject?

Samira nods.

SAMIRA
Drink?

AMIR
God, yes. You know where the cups are.

Samira walks to the kitchen. Grabs two glasses from a cabinet. Returns to the living room and hands Amir a glass.

Whiskey bottle open, she pours into Amir's glass then hers. She lays the bottle on the table in front of them and settles next to Amir on the couch.

SAMIRA
So, are you gonna tell me what brought you back, or do I have to pull it out of you?

Amir is about to answer, when his mobile rings.

AMIR
One sec. I have to take this.

Samira raises an eyebrow as he answers the call.

AMIR
Hello? Yes, this is he… Right now? Sure, no problem at all… You can tell him that I’ll be there as soon as possible… See you then.

Amir hangs up the call. His eyes meet Samira’s inquisitive eyes.

SAMIRA
Amir… What aren’t you telling me?

Amir, still holding Samira’s gaze, shrugs and smiles.

J&R STUDIOS
CREATIVE OFFICES

JJ walks out of the elevator, stride like he's on a mission and time is against him.

The floor is already alive. Controlled chaos.

An argument break out in one of the two-glass walled writer rooms. Producers hover in office doorways, catching people as they pass.

JJ continues past the story editor’s office. Past the pitch room. Past a pair of assistants whispering beside the printer like they’re discussing national secrets. A phone rings unanswered.

Three young women on the brink of a panic attack speed walk past JJ.

WOMAN TEXTING
—meeting was moved to today.

WOMAN HOLDING COFFEE CUP
You can't be serious.

WOMAN HOLDING BINDER
Son of a —

JJ continues to weave his way through the Creative Offices' shared work space: long tables disappear beneath laptops and coffee cups. Overworked notebooks lie open barely alive.

Mark, an recent intern, working at one of the desks, sees JJ walk past. He stands up and rushes to catch up to JJ, leaving all his belongings behind.

MARK
JJ!

JJ keeps walking.

JJ
Not now, Mark.

MARK
But it's really important.

Mark catches up fast, falling into step beside JJ.

JJ
How in the hell —

MARK
I wanted to double check some of the wording in the application announcement.

JJ
Look just write down whatever notes or questions you have and I'll get to them later. I really need to talk to Jess.

Mark nods and heads back to his desk in the open office area as JJ makes his way to

JESS'S OFFICE

JJ doesn't even knock. He simply walks right in.

Jess Njeim stands near one of the walls studying concept art. She looks strikingly like JJ. Same eyes. Same sharp features, they both inherited from their father.

Then again, they are twins. 

JESS
You know one of these days you're going to walk in on me doing something that'll scar you for life.

JJ
Like you'd ever do something in the office.

JESS
Hey, you never know. I might just snap one day and say F it.

Jess turns to face JJ and leans her back against the wall.

JJ
I think dad's going to screw us over. 

JESS
Who and a what now?

JJ
This isn't funny. I think dad is going to hand off NVP to someone else.

JESS
Who?

JJ
Does it matter? This program is ours. We built it from the ground up for God's sake.

JESS
Are you sure you're not overthinking it? You know you have a tendency to spiral.

JJ
I'm not spiraling, Jess. I just left his office and I swear, something just didn't feel right.

Jess walks up to JJ. She crosses her arms. They might be twins, but she was the first out. That's how she ended up with the role of big sister.

JESS
Look if it's bothering you that much, why not just go back up there and ask him?

JJ throws his hands in the air. He's spiraling. Denying it no longer mattered.

JJ
He kicked me out of his office. I can't —

JESS
JJ, breathe. Whatever dad is or isn't doing, I'm sure he has his reasons. If you disagree with them, you have to go up and talk it out. That's the...

JJ rolls his eyes.

JJ
...Njeim way. 

Jess pats JJ on his cheek.

JESS
Keep me updated, would ya?

JJ nods.

EXECUTIVE FLOOR
JAD'S OFFICE

Jad still sits at his desk working through more paperwork. No matter how much he goes through, there's always more.

The office phone rings.

JAD
Yes... Thanks, Carla. Put him through. 

Jad turns to his laptop and opens a document that looks like a treatment.

JAD
Yves! Hello, my friend. I hope you are calling with good news.

YVES
Unfortunately, non mon ami. They would no go for it.

JAD
Damn it.

Jad almost slams his fist onto his desk, but stops himself mere inches above it.

YVES
Listen, give me a few more days and I'll try to secure it. I still have a few other contacts I could reach out to.

JAD
Ok, thank you. I owe you.

YVES
Oui. You do. And you know I'll collect.

They both laugh.

Carla knocks on the office door, then opens it just enough to peek her head through. Knowing that Jad is still on the phone, she mouths the words, "Your appointment is here."

Jad covers the speaker end of the phone with his hand. He mouths back, "Let him in."

JAD
Yves, I'm sorry I have to go, but thank you so much. I'll be in touch soon... Take care.

Jad returns the handset to its cradle. Turns to Carla with a welcoming smile.

JAD
Let him in.

Carla nods and opens the door wider and steps aside.

Behind her stands Amir, who steps into the office with a confidence that almost works. Almost. His shoulders are squared, but his hands don’t seem to know what to do with themselves.

AMIR
Mr. Njeim.

Jad stands and shakes Amir's hand. 

JAD
Please, call me Jad. We've known each other long enough for you to do that. You're practically family.

AMIR
If by family you mean mortal enemies, sure.

JAD
What's family without a few of those? Please, sit.

Amir sits in one of the chairs facing Jad's desk. He looks around the office, observing it. Studying it. How unlike his father's office. Warmer. Calmer. Enough breathing space. A great view.

JAD
Well, I'm sure you want to know why I called you here. I also want to say thank you for taking the trip from the UK, especially with such short notice.

AMIR
Well, you said that it was important, and that I won't regret it.

Jad smiles. 

JAD
Well, I'm not going to waste too much time. I want to offer you a job. Here. At J&R Studios.

Amir half opens his mouth to answer when the office door swings open and JJ walks in.

Amir turns around. JJ sees him. Freezes in the doorway.

AMIR
Hi...

END OF EPISODE 001
