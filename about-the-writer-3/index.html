ABOUT THE WRITER

Alan A. Mehanna is a screenwriter and storyteller based in Lebanon.

He spent years writing for the screen, all while teaching the craft at a university level. Alongside this path, he was a film critic at Lebanon's renowned newspaper, and the creator and co-host of Script2Screen, a podcast focusing on discussing and analyzing the film and television industry.

The Scripts We Inherit is a passion project that grew out of his love for soap-operas and his belief that some stories are simply more fun when you get to live with them for a while.
